## Known gaps / follow-ups

Listed so that none of them reads as covered above. Not part of this feature unless a row says otherwise.

1. **Claude Code's rendering of the transition and site-plan questions is owner-only** (T978). The packaged
   gate uses a scripted elicitation client, so it proves the frame, the answer and the withdrawal, not what the
   person sees. quickstart.md check 1 covers the transition question; nothing owner-side covers `propose_sites`.
2. **The 120 s wait for a late question is unit only** (FR-488, SC-215): router and prompt tests under fake
   time. No packaged or live run waits a question out after a long call (US3's independent test).
3. **A revoke has no packaged or live evidence** (FR-490..FR-493, SC-216): the stores and the composed worker
   through the settings command router are unit-tested; the real settings page is quickstart.md check 2, pending.
4. **The activity has no packaged evidence** (FR-494, SC-217): unit and component tests only; quickstart.md
   check 3 is pending.
5. **A question the host's backstop withdraws adds no activity line** (research R-488, review N3), so SC-217's
   "every question a session settles" does not include it. No test pins the omission.
6. **FR-497 has no 032 test.** It rests on 031's holder and dialog tests, which 032 did not touch, and on the
   statement that no holder survives a restart.
7. **FR-496 is not a new test and is released at start, not after 20 s** (R-489 revised): the evidence is the
   existing start-up reconciliation test; the optional quickstart.md check 4 is the only live look.
8. **The older-host fail-closed behaviour** (R-485 Compatibility: a host that does not know the new kinds asks
   nothing, so the card alone stands) has no test.
9. **Reverse-order one-answer case for the new kinds** (card answers first, then a late client answer) is unit
   only; the packaged gate covers the client-first order.
10. **tasks.md scope note**: T976 names slices A and B, while the review that ran covered A, B and C together.
    Slice D (test-only) had no review, as planned.
11. **Closes two 031 gaps** (see `specs/031-outside-agent-experience/coverage.md`, "Known gaps"): a site revoke
    now clears session grants (031 gap 4 becomes FR-490), and the activity names the channel of a consent answer
    (031 gap 7 becomes FR-494). The transition and site-plan questions are now raised to the client, so 031
    gap 1 applies to the plan card and the upload-directory card only. The other 031 gaps are unchanged.
